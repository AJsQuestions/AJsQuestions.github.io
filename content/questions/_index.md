Questions I keep coming back to.

{{< constellation >}}

- When does uncertainty improve judgment?
- If two people see the same facts and disagree, what is different between them?
- What breaks first when a system scales: trust, information, or incentives?
- How often do systems optimize what is measurable over what matters?
- When does a tool help thinking, and when does it replace it?
- When does a model turn into a belief?
- When does simplification become distortion?
- How many disagreements are really about language?
